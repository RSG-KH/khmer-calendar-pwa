## Install on iPhone or iPad

1. Open [Khmer Calendar](https://rsg-kh.github.io/khmer-calendar-pwa/) in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Enable **Open as Web App** if shown, then tap **Add**.
4. Open **Khmer Calendar** from your Home Screen.

## Install on Mac

Requires **macOS 14 or later**.

1. Open [Khmer Calendar](https://rsg-kh.github.io/khmer-calendar-pwa/) in **Safari**.
2. Choose **File → Add to Dock**, then click **Add**.
3. Open **Khmer Calendar** from your Dock.

Need help? See Apple's guides for [iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-mide/guide/iphone/iphea86e5236/ios), [iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-euro/guide/ipad/ipad8f1f7a29/ipados) and [Mac](https://support.apple.com/en-us/104996).

## Install on Android

For Android phones and tablets:

1. Open [Khmer Calendar](https://rsg-kh.github.io/khmer-calendar-pwa/) in **Chrome**.
2. Tap **⋮ → Install and create shortcut → Install**, then confirm the installation.
3. Open **Khmer Calendar** from your Home Screen or app drawer.

See [Chrome's Android installation guide](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/9658361?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid) for help with the browser's install menu.

## Install on Windows

1. Open [Khmer Calendar](https://rsg-kh.github.io/khmer-calendar-pwa/) in **Microsoft Edge** or **Chrome**.
2. If the **Install app** icon appears in the address bar, click it and confirm **Install**. Otherwise, use the menu steps below.
3. Open **Khmer Calendar** from the Start menu or its app shortcut. You can pin it to your taskbar.

**If the install icon doesn't appear**, install through your browser's menu:

- [Microsoft Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/edge/install-manage-or-uninstall-apps-in-microsoft-edge): **⋯ → More tools → Apps → Install this site as an app**.
- [Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/9658361?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop): **⋮ → Cast, save, and share → Install page as app**.

## Install on Linux

1. Open [Khmer Calendar](https://rsg-kh.github.io/khmer-calendar-pwa/) in **Chrome**.
2. Choose **⋮ → Cast, save, and share → Install page as app**, then confirm **Install**. You can also use the install icon in the address bar when shown.
3. Open **Khmer Calendar** from your applications menu or Chrome's app list at `chrome://apps`.

See [Chrome's desktop installation guide](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/9658361?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop) for help managing the app and its shortcuts.

## Good to know

- Works offline after the first full online load in your browser or installed app.
- Browse years **1800–2200**. Recurring observances and holy days are calculated on-device; official public-holiday calendars cover **2016–2027**, and date-backed historical milestones appear at their recorded dates (**1886–2025** in catalog 0.5.0).
- Commemorations show their anniversary count in both languages — ខួបលើកទី៤៧ in Khmer and **· 47th** with English ordinal suffixes.
- Every catalog event carries a curated bilingual knowledge summary: tap an event, then **Learn more**. **Ask AI** / **សួរ AI** opens your browser on a Google AI-mode search for that event only when you tap it.
- Your events and settings stay on your device. No account or sync; clearing app/site data removes them.
- Personal events can repeat every X days, weekly, monthly or yearly, with a required end date and a preview. Choose how to handle missing month-end or leap-day dates; editing or deleting applies to the whole series.
- To update, tap **Check for update** in Settings. Available updates download and reload automatically; saved events and settings are kept.
- Swipe left or right on the calendar to change months.
- Tap the month to choose a month and year, then **Go**. **This year** changes the draft year and keeps your chosen month.
- In the calendar header, **Today** returns to today's date and reflects selection status dynamically (neutral text when today is selected, and accent highlight when viewing other dates). In the calendar grid, Today stays filled; another selected date has an outline.
- The calendar month card includes an adaptive legend for holidays, Buddhist holy days, observances, and personal events.
- Calendar width stays steady when switching between four-, five- and six-row months. On tablets and desktops in landscape, monthly events fill the remaining space up to twice the calendar width, with gaps at the sides.
- Settings includes background tint, Light/Dark choices, 80%–150% app font size, longer weekday names, weekday colors, a global observances switch, and Western zodiac and Chinese Ganzhi controls.
- Date details show the Western Big 3 (Sun, Moon and Rising sign) and Ganzhi year, month, day and hour signs with clash animals. When either table is on, the popup starts with the current time from **Today follows** when its date is Today in that zone; the Rising place never auto-shifts the visible clock. Supported past and future dates start at the saved 24-hour default time (initially 12:00), which can be changed for the open date through the header button. You can edit that time. Big 3 uses the date shown in the popup title and its displayed time, resolved in the chosen place's time zone with that place's coordinates. Each table can be turned off independently; Ganzhi solar year and month signs cover 1900–2100, while day and hour signs cover 1800–2200.
- Tap anywhere on either table, including its headings, to open **Zodiac · Big 3** or **Chinese Ganzhi (干支)**. Keyboard users can focus the table and press Enter or Space. The popup keeps the same values and emoji preference, with a Sun-sign or Year-animal background. Big 3 adds three compact sign, element and planet rows from the bundled zodiac catalog. **Ask AI** searches for the displayed signs and pillars without including the selected date, time, place or coordinates. **Close** returns to the unchanged date details; horizontal table scrolling remains available on narrow screens.
- In date details, choose a place for Rising sign from an offline administrative catalog. Cambodia uses province/capital → district/municipality/khan → commune/sangkat with Khmer and English names; all 1,663 communes/sangkats now have reference coordinates, including 432 newly filled points. Other countries use GeoNames ADM1 → ADM2 → ADM3 points, with selection possible at any available level. The last two levels share one search field: results show `ADM 2 · ADM 3`, and its suggestions open without typing. The catalog includes 201,002 GeoNames administrative divisions in 227 countries plus 1,898 Cambodian divisions, and is about 4.7 MB compressed. Saved place chips and older GeoNames city selections remain usable. For a more precise birthplace, use **Custom location** to enter latitude, longitude and an IANA time zone. The selected time zone alone never supplies a birth location.
- Buddhist Holy Day and Shaving Day markers appear directly below the full lunar date, above the divider and astrology tables in date details.
- In Settings > Astrology & Zodiac, **Enable astrology and zodiac** is on by default. Turning it off hides the section's other settings and stops Western zodiac and Ganzhi calculations throughout the app; turning it back on restores the saved individual choices. **Set time for past and future** opens a time-only picker, and **Set location for rising sign** opens a location-only picker; its initial place is Sangkat Voat Phnum in Khan Doun Penh, Phnom Penh Capital, Cambodia. With Big 3 enabled, the date-details header button shows the selected country's flag and opens **Time and location for Big 3 & Ganzhi**; a custom place without a known country shows a location marker. With Big 3 off and Ganzhi on, the button keeps the clock emoji and opens a compact **Select time** popup. Date-details changes do not change the Settings defaults. In the combined popup, use **Custom location** to enter coordinates and search for a time zone, or tap a saved custom-location chip to fill its fields. Tap a chip's × to delete it immediately. **Pick location** returns to the searchable catalog. **Cancel** discards draft time and location changes; **Save** applies them together.
- Scheduled reminders are not available in PWA mode.

You can also use the calendar directly in your browser without installing it.

## About the project

This is the web version of [Khmer Calendar for Android](https://github.com/RSG-KH/khmer-calendar). Both apps use [Khmer Calendar Engine](https://github.com/RSG-KH/khmer-calendar-engine) for calendar calculations. Calendar algorithms, source references and validation are documented in the [engine project](https://github.com/RSG-KH/khmer-calendar-engine#verification-and-accuracy).

For setup, testing and hosting, see [DEVELOPMENT.md](DEVELOPMENT.md). Dependency and event catalog updates are covered in the [engine integration guide](docs/shared-engine.md). The calendar grid, summary, Western Big 3 and Ganzhi tables have a [maintainer-certified PWA layout contract](docs/maintainer-certified-calendar-ui.md) for future Android syncs.
