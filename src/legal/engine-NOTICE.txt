Khmer Calendar lunar arithmetic is adapted from:
https://github.com/MetheaX/khmer-chhankitek-calendar
Commit: 4d1df001de73fac715d6e4c54f06f69618ff49dd
Based on the work of Phylypo Tum and Thyrith Sor (momentkh).
The shared Kotlin implementation uses immutable month indexing, Gregorian calendar years,
and Buddhist Era rollover at the start of 1 Roach of Pisakh.
