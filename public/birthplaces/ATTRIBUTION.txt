The primary reference for Cambodia administrative names, codes and hierarchy is the Wikipedia administrative tables listed below. The catalog also retains some additional commune records and name variants from CambodiaPostalCode, used as a supplemental and verification reference. The saved comparison found 11 commune records absent from the Wikipedia snapshots and 27 shared-code records with differing fields; those differences are not a certification that every retained entry is a current administrative unit. No blanket redistribution license is asserted for the imported source bundle; see its NOTICE.md for its own provenance account.

Cambodia administrative name, hierarchy and geocode comparisons: Wikipedia contributors, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Cambodia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_districts,_municipalities_and_sections_in_Cambodia, and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_communes_in_Cambodia. Applicable Wikimedia attribution and share-alike terms remain with that material.

Cambodia original division coordinates (1,453 records): Open Admin Data, https://openadmindata.org/api/kh/ and https://github.com/open-admin-data/cambodia-administrative-divisions, CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. Coordinates were joined by administrative geocode.

Cambodia supplemental coordinates (445 previously empty records): the reviewed missing_coordinates_filled_bundle. Its source fields credit OCHA / Cambodia Department of Geography administrative boundaries (357 points derived as 2018 ADM3 polygon centroids), plus GeoNames, OpenStreetMap / Nominatim, Mapcarta and Wikidata reference points and several explicitly marked representative fallbacks. See public/birthplaces/provenance.json for the bundle fingerprint and method counts. Six filled points have low confidence. These points are approximate administrative or locality references, not exact birth addresses or verified current boundary centroids. The upstream source names and methods are retained in the input bundle; no single license is asserted for all supplemental material.

Other countries' ADM1, ADM2 and ADM3 names, hierarchy codes, points and time zones: GeoNames, https://www.geonames.org/ and https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/, CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. The app filters the gazetteer to these three administrative feature codes; no town or village records are included in the current catalog.
